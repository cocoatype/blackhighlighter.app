01 · About

What Black Highlighter does.

Two paragraphs. Lift them straight into your piece.

Black Highlighter hides text you don't want to share, in pictures you do. It's a redaction app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac that uses on-device machine learning to find names, emails, account numbers, and anything else in an image, and cover them up with neat black bars.

The redactions are flat pixels in the exported image. Nothing leaves your device. There's no subscription, no account, no cloud. Made by Geoff Pado, an indie developer in Seattle, under the name Cocoatype.

02 · Key features

Six things to mention.

The first three ship with the free app. The last three unlock with the in-app purchase.

Free

Magic Highlighter

Snaps to detected text and covers it completely. Bars come out neat and even — and harder to guess at, because letter shapes don't show through.

Free

Color Picker

Use different colors to differentiate redactions — handy for distinguishing speakers in a chat thread.

Free

iOS, iPadOS & macOS

One purchase, three platforms. Edit on whichever device you're on.

Paid

Automatic Redaction

Save a list of words and phrases. Open an image and Black Highlighter finds and hides them for you — every time.

Paid

Workflow Integration

Hooks into Shortcuts on iOS and Automator on macOS so you can pipe images through redaction in larger workflows.

Paid

Document Scanner

Scan a paper document with the same tech that powers Apple Notes — auto-cropped, deskewed, and ready to redact.

All paid features unlock with subscription or one-time in-app purchase.
