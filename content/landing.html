The Headline Feature

Magic Highlighter snaps to text — and covers it perfectly.

Drag a stroke across any name, email, or account number. Black Highlighter detects the bounds and lays down a clean, opaque bar. Neater than a finger, and harder to guess at than wobbly hand-drawn rectangles.

Frequently Asked

Questions worth answering before you download

Is the redaction permanent?

Yes. When you export the redacted image, the pixels under the bars are flattened; there's no hidden layer to peel back. Saved files contain only the redacted pixels.

Does Black Highlighter upload my photos anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. The app does not connect to any server, and works fully offline.

Will the redaction survive screenshots and re-shares?

Yes. Once exported, the bars are baked into the image. Forwarding, screenshotting, or printing it is fully redacted.

Can someone reverse the redaction?

Black Highlighter uses fully opaque solid-color bars (not blur or pixelation) so there is no statistical signal left to reverse-engineer.

Does it work on languages other than English?

Yes. The Magic Highlighter snaps to text in any language Apple's on-device OCR supports, including non-Latin scripts.

How much does it cost?

Black Highlighter is free to download. A one-time purchase or subscription (your choice!) unlocks the document scanner, automatic rules, and Shortcuts integration.
