Black Highlighter 24.5 makes a few minor improvements:

- The photo library remembers what album you had previously selected across launches, so if you store photos to be selected in a given album, you don't have to go searching.
- Shortcuts that add redactions now allow you to specify the color of the redaction.
- The page for marketing Ultra Highlighter is now easier to use.
