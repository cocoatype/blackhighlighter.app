Black Highlighter 2025.2 adds several ways to quickly get to the document scanner, expands Shortcuts integration, and improves the purchase experience.

## Document Scanner Quick Control

Add Black Highlighter's document scanner to Control Center for easy access. A quick tap opens Black Highlighter ready to scan documents for redaction.

## Document Scanner Shortcuts Action

A new "Open Document Scanner" Shortcuts action opens Black Highlighter's document scanner, perfect for workflows that need to capture and redact documents.

## Auto-Hidden Words in Redaction Action

The unified "Redact Image" Shortcuts action now includes an option to use your auto-hidden words list for redaction. Automatically redact any words from your custom list without specifying them each time.

## Improved Paywall Design

The subscription screen shows clearer information about available free trials and pricing options.

## Bug Fixes

- Purchasing errors that previously failed silently now show a helpful message.
- Fixed an issue where canceled purchases prevented starting a new purchase without closing and reopening the purchase screen.
