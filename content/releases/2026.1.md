## What’s New
- Black Highlighter does a better job finding what you want hidden. It reads your whole screenshot at once, so multi-line addresses and names on their own line (chat headers, “From:” lines, contact cards) are much more likely to be caught.

## Bug Fixes
- The Auto-Hidden Words screen slides in smoothly, with the navigation bar arriving right along with everything else.
- I updated error reporting to leave out device identifiers and file names, to continue protecting your privacy.
