Black Highlighter 2025.5 builds in support for Apple's September 2025 releases with a whole bunch of new features:

## Liquid Glass Design

Black Highlighter fully embraces the Liquid Glass design aesthetic of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS 26. Liquid Glass has been fully baked into all parts of Black Highlighter, with many design improvements throughout the app.

## Recognize Text Shortcuts Action

A new Shortcuts action, "Recognize Text", finds and returns all text in an image. Use this action alongside the new system "Use Model" action to safely use AI models to help with redaction.

## Open Recent on iPad

The iPad version of Black Highlighter supports opening recent files and images, directly from the home screen.

## Better macOS Zoom Controls

Zoom in and out of your image on macOS, even without a trackpad, using new zoom controls.
