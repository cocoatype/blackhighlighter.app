## iPhone Duo Support
- Introducing support for iPhone Duo! Black Highlighter is built to look and work great on Apple’s newest iPhone. Open any image on your iPhone Duo to get started.

## Bug Fixes
- Auto-hidden words redact only the lines that contain a matching name, address, or phone number, so images with no matches open without unsaved edits.
- Settings displays its title in the correct font.
- Navigation titles display in the correct color in light mode.
